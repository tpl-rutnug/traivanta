What we run for you

Systems for employing people in another country, handled end to end. Strategy and advice when you are deciding how to grow there.

  • Employer of RecordEOR

    We become the legal employer for your team abroad: contracts, onboarding, payroll and statutory obligations. You direct the work, we carry the employment responsibilities.

  • Professional Employer OrganizationPEO

    Shared employment for companies that already have a local presence and want HR, benefits and compliance managed by specialists.

  • Agency of RecordAOR

    A managed route for working with independent professionals: agreements, invoicing, payments and tax documentation in one place.

  • Payroll, tax and benefits

    Accurate pay runs, tax compliance, payslips and benefits administration, with clear monthly reporting for your finance team.

  • Cross-border labour compliance

    We track the rules that apply to your people in each jurisdiction and keep your employment practices inside them as laws change.

  • Management consultancy

    Advice on organizational development, training programmes and the structure of your international operations, including partnerships with global entities.

How an engagement works

A clear path from first call to steady monthly operations.

  1. Scope

    We learn where your people are, what they do and which model fits: EOR, PEO or AOR.

  2. Set up

    Agreements, registrations and payroll are prepared and checked before anyone starts.

  3. Run

    We handle pay, taxes, benefits and filings every month, on schedule.

  4. Report

    You get plain statements of cost and compliance status, and a named contact for questions.
